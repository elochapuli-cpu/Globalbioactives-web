The origin

Extreme adaptation. Extraordinary bioactivity.

Porphyridium cruentum is a marine microalga that has evolved over millions of years to survive extreme environments: high radiation, salinity variations, dehydration and oxidative stress.

This unique adaptive capacity activates natural protection, repair and defence mechanisms, giving rise to extraordinary bioactives with exceptional benefits for the skin. AcquaSea® transforms this biological intelligence into precision marine biotechnology for cosmetics.

The molecule

The biological advantage behind superior hydration.

Hyaluronic acid set the standard for hydration. But modern skin needs more than water retention. AcquaSea® transforms hydration into a complete biological response.

1

Superior molecular architecture

  • Composed of six different monosaccharides.
  • 3.5–7% natural sulphation.
  • Broad molecular-weight spectrum: 140–6,000 kDa.
  • Complex, branched and well-balanced structure.
2

Key differential sulphation

  • Greater water-binding capacity.
  • Resistant to hyaluronidase.
  • Greater stability and long-lasting effect.
  • Greater affinity for cellular receptors.
  • Mimics endogenous GAGs.
  • Modulates FGF and TGF-β growth factors.
3

Multifunctional biological activity

  • Provides intensive hydration across multiple skin layers.
  • Biostimulates the skin: fibroblasts, endogenous HA and collagen.
  • Protects against oxidative stress, pollution and blue light.
  • Soothes and strengthens the skin barrier.
  • Improves firmness, elasticity and radiance.
