The potential of microalgae transformed into natural, sustainable and functional bioactives.

GlobalBioActives is a family of 100% natural bioactives obtained from microalgae and developed to deliver functionality, differentiation and added value to products in cosmetics, human and animal nutrition, supplementation, agriculture and aquaculture.

We combine the biological diversity of microalgae with proprietary cultivation and processing technology to obtain high-quality, reproducible bioactives adapted to the needs of each application.

100% natural and sustainable

  • Cyanobacteria—traditionally included among microalgae—were among the first unicellular organisms to inhabit the planet. Over billions of years, their photosynthetic activity played a decisive role in transforming the atmosphere and making life as we know it possible.
  • Microalgae are major oxygen producers and play an essential role in the oceans’ ability to act as the planet’s main natural CO₂ sink.
  • They form the base of the food chain and are among the most efficient photosynthetic organisms on the planet.

About us

Technology, corporate structure and industrial capacity integrated into one project.

GlobalBioActives brings together intellectual property, specialised microalgae expertise, operating capability and its own industrial facility. The structure clearly shows the role of each entity.

Holding company and intellectual property
NBA Biotech Limited

NBA Biotech Limited is the group’s parent and holding company. It owns the patents and intellectual property, oversees the other companies and coordinates the group’s activities.

Commercial brand

GlobalBioActives is the commercial family under which our natural bioactives for cosmetics, nutrition, supplementation, agriculture and aquaculture are presented and marketed.

Industrial facility

Natur Bio Actives Alicante is the 3,000 m² industrial facility in Agost, Alicante, dedicated to controlled microalgae cultivation and the production of high-value natural bioactives.

Technology and operations

Global Biotech S.L. is the technology company that developed the cultivation and processing technology, designed and built the plant, and operates it for NBA Biotech.

Our proposition

A family of bioactives built on science, sustainability and adaptability.

Microalgae are one of the most promising sources of functional compounds for industry. Their richness in proteins, pigments, polysaccharides, antioxidants and essential fatty acids enables the development of highly functional bioactives for a wide range of markets.

  • 100% natural bioactives of microalgal origin.
  • Solutions tailored to each customer and application.
  • High bioavailability and absorption of functional compounds.
  • Suitable for vegan and sustainability-led propositions.
  • Science-based development and controlled production processes.
Technology

Designed to transform microalgal biology into high-quality industrial bioactives.

Our technology combines Closed System, control and automation of critical parameters, sustainable processes and industrial scale-up. The objective is to transform the biological potential of microalgae into reproducible, safe and functional bioactives.

Processing, purification and stabilisationDownstream infrastructure to transform microalgal biomass into high-value bioactives.
Industrial infrastructureAuxiliary equipment, treatment and process support integrated into a scalable platform.
Centralised monitoringReal-time visualisation of critical cultivation variables and production-system status.
Actuator automationOperational management of the greenhouse’s key elements.
01

Control and automation

Continuous monitoring of temperature, pH, oxygen, nutrients and other critical variables to ensure the optimum environment for each species.

02

Closed System

Protection against external contaminants, higher culture purity and more stable, safe and traceable production.

03

Sustainable process

We prioritise physical separation and purification, efficient water use and full utilisation of biomass.

04

Circular economy

Resource optimisation, stream reuse and process design aligned with sustainability and industrial efficiency.

05

Industrial production

Automated, scalable processes designed to meet commercial demand while maintaining quality.

06

Tailored solutions

Adjustment of concentrations, formats, applications and technical positioning to maximise the value of each bioactive.

07

Patent-protected system

The production system is protected by two patent families, strengthening the group’s technological differentiation and intellectual property.

08

Modular and easily scalable system

The plant’s hydraulic and pneumatic design groups production units into fully homogeneous reactors that can be replicated modularly, like building blocks, as production requirements increase. The current plant comprises 512 production units grouped into 16 reactors.

Featured cosmetic active

AcquaSea®: one molecule, four biological mechanisms.

Within the GlobalBioActives family, AcquaSea® is the flagship active. It is a sulphated extracellular polysaccharide obtained from the marine microalga Porphyridium cruentum, designed to provide advanced hydration, biostimulation, PDRN-like regeneration and active protection in a single ingredient.
